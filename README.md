# 🚗 WTF did I park?: Your Ultimate Parking Assistant! 🚗

Tired of wandering around huge parking lots trying to remember where you left your car? Say hello to **WTF did I park?** – your one-stop app for never losing your vehicle again, right from your browser! 🚀

WTF did I park? is a sleek, intuitive, and lightning-fast tool that simplifies your parking experience.

Visit [WTF did I park?](https://wtfdidipark.jan-hafner.de) now!

## 🌟 Features You'll Love

- **Pin Your Car:** Save your car's exact location with a single tap. Stop searching! 📍
- **Real-Time Tracking:** Instantly see your current position and distance to your car. 🚶‍♂️
- **Compass & Map Rotation:** Navigate back to your car easily with a built-in compass and rotating map functionality. 🧭
- **Accuracy Visualizer:** Know exactly how precise your GPS location is. 🎯
- **Installable App (PWA):** For a native-app-like experience, you can install WTF did I park? directly to your desktop or mobile home screen. It's fast, reliable, and works offline! 💻📱
- **Sleek & Modern UI:** Enjoy a beautiful, user-friendly interface built with Vuetify, featuring both Light and Dark themes. 🎨

## 🚀 Why Choose WTF did I park?

- **Save Time:** Spend less time hunting for your car and more time doing what matters.
- **Always Available:** It's always ready to go, right from your browser or home screen.
- **Reliable & Accurate:** Get precise locations every single time using your device's GPS.
- **Open Source:** The code is open for you to explore, customize, and contribute to!

## 💡 Get Started!

Using WTF did I park? is a breeze. Just open the application, grant location permissions, tap to save your car's location when you park, and let the app guide you back later! ✨

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Vue.js:** For a reactive and efficient user interface.
- **Vuetify:** A stunning Material Design component framework.
- **Leaflet:** For interactive maps and geolocation features.
- **TypeScript:** For robust and scalable code.
- **Vite:** For a blazing-fast development and build experience.
- **Pinia:** For state management.
